System Architecture & Acoustics

How VoiceClone Scans Speech in Your Browser

VoiceClone combines the Web Audio API, WebGPU / WebAssembly, and fine-tuned wav2vec2 transformer representations to inspect vocal dynamics directly inside your browser memory.

The 4-Step Analysis Pipeline

Every byte of audio is processed locally on your device in real time.

1

Raw PCM Ingestion

Audio is captured via an AudioWorklet or decoded directly from uploaded files. All browser digital signal processing (echo cancellation, noise suppression, AGC) is turned off to ensure fine-grained vocoder artifacts are not erased by operating system filters.

2

16 kHz Resampling & Quality Checks

An OfflineAudioContext resamples the audio into a clean, single-channel 16,000 Hz Float32Array. Before classification, the audio passes rigorous quality gating: checking for clipping distortion, excessive silence, whisper volume, and background noise.

3

Neural Transformer Window Scan

Audio is segmented into 4.0-second sliding windows with 1.0-second overlap (up to 15 windows). A quantized wav2vec2 neural network parses raw speech waveforms into multi-layer latent representations to search for synthetic speech generation traces.

4

Probabilistic Synthesis & Verdict

The system aggregates window predictions into mean, peak, and threshold metrics. Results are mapped to calibrated probability bands (<35% Human, 35-65% Inconclusive, >65% AI), capping displayed certainty at 95% to maintain scientific honesty.

Browser Processing Flow

Zero data transfer: everything stays inside the sandboxed browser execution context.

Why AI Voices Leave Traces

Modern voice cloning systems (such as ElevenLabs, XTTS, Bark, and VALL-E) generally rely on two core components: an acoustic model that converts text to mel-spectrogram frames, and a neural vocoder (like HiFi-GAN or diffusion upsamplers) that converts spectrograms into audible sound waves. This process leaves distinct physical imperfections:

Natural Human Speech

  • Organic Micro-Tremor: Natural vocal cords constantly exhibit subtle frequency variations (jitter) and amplitude variations (shimmer) caused by muscular respiration.
  • Formant Damping: The throat, tongue, and nasal cavities dynamically shape and dampen harmonic frequencies across vowels and consonants.
  • Acoustic Room Tone: Even in silent pauses, air molecules, ambient room reverberation, and inhale sounds are present.

Synthetic AI Speech Traces

  • Vocoder Phase Inconsistencies: Neural vocoders construct waveforms frame-by-frame, frequently creating microscopic phase misalignments at frame boundaries.
  • Pitch Unnaturalness: Synthesized sustained vowels often display mathematically perfect pitch lines without natural biological drift.
  • Digital Silence & High-Frequency Noise: Pauses in generated audio often drop to absolute mathematical zeros or exhibit high-frequency checkerboard harmonics above 7.5 kHz.

100% Privacy by Design

Voice biometric data is intensely sensitive. If a scammer has sent you a voice message or you record a conversation, sending that file to a third-party cloud server introduces severe privacy, surveillance, and legal risks.

VoiceClone was engineered from the ground up as a pure client-side static application. The neural network weights are downloaded into your browser cache on first use, after which the entire inference runs via WebGPU or WebAssembly. Disconnecting your internet connection after the model is downloaded will not stop the detector from functioning.
